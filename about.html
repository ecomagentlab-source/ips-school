About Us

Education and character-building together

Quality, Inspiration & Innovation

IPS Inspire Public School Kamber

IPS Inspire Public School & The Scientific Academy — English Coaching Centre is located at Mastoi Chowk, Meerokhan Road, Kamber (Qambar), Sindh. We offer schooling from Nursery to Class 8, Hifz and Nazra classes, and coaching in English, Science and Mathematics.

Our motto is “Quality, Inspiration & Innovation”. We believe in combining worldly and religious education with strong character-building — تعليم و تربيت گڏوگڏ.
